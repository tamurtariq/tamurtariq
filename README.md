## 💡 How I work

- **Human in the loop by default.** AI drafts and recommends; people approve anything high-impact.
- **Start with the workflow, not the model.** I map the process first, automate the boring 80%, and only add AI where it earns its keep.
- **Production-minded.** Logging, retries, secrets management, cost limits and monitoring are part of the build, not an afterthought.
- **Clear scope and communication.** Fixed deliverables, short feedback loops and documentation you can hand to your team.
